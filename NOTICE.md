## What Hangar does with your RSI account

- It reads only your own pledges page and Hangar log, in a browser window you sign in to yourself (or, with the
  bookmark, in your own browser). It changes nothing on RSI and buys, melts, gifts or upgrades nothing.
- What it reads goes only into Hangar on your computer. Nothing is sent to anyone else, and there is no telemetry.
- Your RSI password is typed into RSI's own page. Hangar never sees or stores it. The browser window keeps its own
  sign-in in Hangar's data folder ("Forget my RSI sign-in" deletes it).
